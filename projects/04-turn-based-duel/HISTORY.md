# 04 — Turn-based duel history

## Chronological log

- 2026-11-02: After completing Crate-pushing and reflecting on the appeal of progressively more complicated concepts, the learner selected a tiny turn-based duel over an undo extension. Created the chronologically numbered notes folder and made it the active project; this does not name or create the learner's Godot source project. The general scope is one small grid-based room, one player, one enemy, and placeholder visuals, with turn state, reusable actors, and simple enemy decisions as learning goals. Proposed a deterministic 3-HP, 1-damage baseline and assigned an action-by-action paper trace for four right-direction commands, including one-action enemy turns and immediate defeat handling. Detailed rules and the trace remain unconfirmed/pending. No Godot setup or game implementation is assigned or performed.
